# Scarlet Sync: why I built it

Draft for Akash to review and record. About 55–65 seconds at a conversational pace.

> I started Scarlet Sync because planning classes at Rutgers could feel like piecing together a puzzle across different websites. I wanted a clearer way to see my options and understand how they fit together.
>
> During development, we spoke with many Rutgers students. Those conversations helped us look beyond our own experience and shaped how we thought about the product.
>
> Scarlet Sync brings course search, schedule building, degree planning, professor information, and course alerts into one place. The idea is to help students go from “What should I take?” to a weekly plan they can actually compare and understand.
>
> For me, the biggest lesson has been that useful software starts with listening. Building the features matters, but so does understanding the people using them and continuing to improve as we learn.
